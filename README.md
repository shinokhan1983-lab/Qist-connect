Qist Connect

Installment-Based Trading & Business Marketplace

Qist Connect is a digital marketplace designed to connect customers with verified installment shopkeepers and dealers.

Our Mission

To make buying products on easy installments simple, transparent, and accessible.

How It Works

1. Customers submit product requests.
2. Registered shopkeepers receive customer requests.
3. Shopkeepers submit their installment offers.
4. Customers compare offers and choose the best option.
5. Successful deals are tracked through Qist Connect.
